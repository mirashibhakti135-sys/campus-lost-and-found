A simple place to report and reclaim what campus loses.

Report a lost or found item in under a minute, search what other students have logged, and submit a claim once you find a match. Every claim is checked by an admin before an item is marked returned. Data is stored permanently in MongoDB, not just this browser.

How it works

1. ReportEnter details of a lost or found item
2. ListedIt's added to the shared database
3. SearchOthers browse and find a match
4. ClaimThe finder or owner submits a claim
5. VerifyAn admin checks the claim
6. ReturnedItem is marked returned to its owner
